# Support

This is a solo project, maintained in spare time. Issues and pull requests
are welcome; responses are best-effort and can take a while.

- **Bug reports:** include the exact command, the seed, your platform, and
  the output. Seeded repro steps get fixed first.
- **Supported setup:** the pinned versions in the requirements files, on the
  platforms the CI runs. Anything else may well work, but it is untested.
- **The stories behind these systems:**
  [the research notebooks](https://stonej-gh.github.io/research/).
